🎮 How to Play

  • 🪽 Press SPACE or ↑ Arrow to fly.
  • 🖱️ Click anywhere on the game canvas to flap.
  • 🚧 Avoid hitting the green pipes.
  • 🌱 Don't fall on the ground.
  • 🏆 Earn one point for every pipe you pass.
  • 💀 If you crash, press SPACE or click Restart.
